# Surface brief: portfolio home (app/page.tsx)

Mode: Experience. Audience: recruiters and freelance clients, equal. Actions: open resume, email.
Proof: projects and experience from content/portfolio.json only. User pinned the world after reviewing v1: reference is jsmastery.com GSAP animations course page (beige/green ground, pinned scroll-scrubbed story text, colourful cards entering from top/side/bottom, stacked project cards). This direction replaces the transit-diagram world and is user-pinned, so no roll.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A warm storybook scroll. The page makes you stop and read (pinned text that lights up word by word), shows work as full-size colourful cards that stack, and tells the career as one winding road that draws itself. It refuses the straight rail line, station signs, and the hero-plus-card-grid template.

OWN-WORLD: Sage-beige ground (#E8EFE4) alternating with signal green (#33C791) and deep ink (#020013); card colours cream #FFE3AC, pink #FFD3FE, lavender #D3D3FF, sky #B8E3E9 with blue #0D8DFF and orange #FE5619 as sparks. Heavy condensed Archivo caps for statements, Figtree for reading. Rounded 28-36px cards with 2-3px ink outlines and soft shadows, no hard offset shadows.

STORY: In seconds the visitor knows who Shivani is (developer with a product brain), sees real products immediately, is made to pause on her story, then follows her career down a winding road and lands on contact cards. They open the resume or email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Beige ground. Left: display headline "I design, build, and ship.", a line naming Shivani Rai as software developer with a product brain, CTAs (See my work, Resume, Take the tour) and open-to-work status. Right: three project screenshot cards tilted in a fan, entering from top, right and bottom, floating and parallaxing to the pointer, with a rotating "open to work" badge. Ink skills marquee below. Conductor guide Tika bottom right.

FORM: Storybook scroll, user-pinned (reference: jsmastery GSAP course page). seed: none (user-pinned)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance


## Update (latest user direction)
Quiet minimalism overrides the earlier loudness: single cream ground #faf4ec, soft ink #262626, borderless pastel cards, light Geist type, no dark outlines or decorative underlines, no marquee, no floating objects. The Tika guide and tour were removed. The pinned block-to-text Story replicates the jsmastery reference exactly and stays.
